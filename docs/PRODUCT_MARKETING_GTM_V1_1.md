## 1. Product positioning

### Brand promise

> **Belajar lebih terarah. Hadapi ujian lebih siap.**

### Product differentiator

> **Bukan cuma latihan soal. SiapTempur membantu kamu tahu apa yang harus dipelajari berikutnya.**

### Product loop

> Latihan → ukur kemampuan → temukan kelemahan → perbaiki → ulangi.

The marketing promise must match the actual product experience. Results and dashboards therefore need to lead users toward a next learning action rather than stopping at score display.

---

## 2. Audience

### CPNS/SKD

Primary segments:

1. first-time CPNS/SKD participants
2. repeat participants who need structured improvement
3. users who already practice questions but do not know their weak areas
4. users looking for realistic timed practice and result analysis

Core anxieties:

- tidak tahu mulai dari mana
- sudah banyak latihan tetapi skor tidak naik
- sulit mengetahui materi yang paling lemah
- takut tidak terbiasa dengan tekanan waktu
- bingung memilih latihan setelah melihat hasil

### TKA SMP

Primary audiences:

1. SMP students preparing for TKA
2. parents helping children prepare
3. students needing structured practice and review

Core needs:

- latihan yang mudah dipahami
- melihat perkembangan
- belajar dari kesalahan
- tahu materi yang perlu diperbaiki
- pengalaman yang tidak terasa rumit/teknis

Marketing tone for students and parents may need different campaign copy even when they enter the same product.

---

## 3. Messaging hierarchy

### Level 1 — problem

> Banyak latihan belum tentu membuat persiapan lebih terarah.

### Level 2 — promise

> SiapTempur membantu kamu melihat kemampuan dan menentukan fokus belajar berikutnya.

### Level 3 — mechanism

> Kerjakan latihan atau tryout, lihat hasilnya, pelajari pembahasan, lalu fokus pada bagian yang masih lemah.

### Level 4 — features

Only after value is understood:

- latihan fokus
- mini tryout
- full tryout
- pembahasan
- riwayat hasil
- perkembangan
- soal yang perlu diulang
- latihan perbaikan
- event tryout

---

## 4. CPNS messaging

### Hero direction

> **Persiapan SKD yang tidak berhenti di skor.**

Supporting:

> Latihan TWK, TIU, dan TKP, ukur kemampuanmu, lalu fokus memperbaiki bagian yang masih lemah.

### Campaign angles

**Weakness discovery**

> Skor tryout cuma angka kalau kamu tidak tahu apa yang harus diperbaiki.

**Consistency**

> Tidak perlu belajar semuanya sekaligus. Mulai dari bagian yang paling membutuhkan perhatian.

**Timed preparation**

> Biasakan mengambil keputusan di bawah batas waktu sebelum hari ujian.

**Review loop**

> Jangan berhenti setelah melihat skor. Pelajari salahnya, perbaiki, lalu coba lagi.

All claims about CAT/BKN similarity, question volume, passing grade, prediction, or pass rate must be verified before use.

---

## 5. TKA SMP messaging

### Hero direction

> **Belajar dari setiap hasil, bukan sekadar menyelesaikan soal.**

Supporting:

> Latihan dan tryout membantu kamu melihat bagian yang sudah kuat dan yang masih perlu diperbaiki sebelum ujian.

### Student angle

> Belajar lebih jelas: kerjakan, lihat hasil, perbaiki bagian yang masih sulit.

### Parent angle

> Bantu anak berlatih lebih terarah dengan hasil dan perkembangan yang mudah dipahami.

Do not use internal educational analytics terminology unless it is explained naturally.

## 6. Acquisition funnel

```text
Short-form content / SEO / Referral / Event
                 ↓
        Problem-specific landing page
                 ↓
           Free useful action
                 ↓
          Result / mini insight
                 ↓
              Register
                 ↓
       Personalized learning loop
                 ↓
        Paid access recommendation
                 ↓
              Payment
                 ↓
             Activation
                 ↓
        Retention / referral
```

### Principle

The free step must produce genuine value.

Examples:

- short diagnostic
- mini tryout
- focused drill
- score + simple weakness insight

Avoid asking users to register/pay before they understand what SiapTempur does unless authentication is genuinely required for the task.

## 7. Landing-page architecture

Recommended main landing structure:

1. Hero: problem + promise + primary CTA
2. Product mechanism: how SiapTempur works
3. Choose CPNS / TKA
4. Product screenshot / result-to-action demonstration
5. Why ordinary question practice is not enough
6. Feature-to-benefit mapping
7. Proof / trust
8. Pricing / access
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA

### Primary CTA

CTA should lead to a useful first action, not merely a product description page when a better action is available.

Possible future CTA:

> `Coba latihan gratis`

or

> `Coba mini tryout`

Only use `gratis` when the linked experience is actually free without hidden access requirements.

Recommended pattern:

```text
Hook
↓
Problem/question
↓
Useful explanation
↓
Mini proof/demo
↓
Low-friction CTA
```

Example CPNS:

> Kamu selalu kehabisan waktu di TIU?
>
> Jangan mulai dengan soal paling panjang. Gunakan putaran pertama untuk mengamankan soal yang bisa kamu jawab cepat.
>
> Coba simulasi waktunya di SiapTempur.

Avoid generic CTAs like `cek link di bio` when a specific benefit can be stated.

## 10. Trust system

Potential trust elements, only when factual:

- transparent question count and format
- clear scoring explanation
- secure payment messaging in plain language
- real participant/user numbers
- real testimonials with permission
- event documentation
- sample result/report
- clear support channel
- refund/terms visibility where relevant

Never fabricate testimonials, counters, rankings, success rates, or institutional affiliations.

## 12. Lifecycle / retention

Potential lifecycle moments:

- account created but no practice started
- first practice completed
- first tryout completed
- weak area identified
- several days inactive
- payment pending
- access activated
- access nearing expiration
- event registration
- improvement milestone

Messages should bring users back to a meaningful action, not merely announce that the product exists.

---

## 13. Referral direction

Referral should be designed after the core learning loop is valuable enough to recommend.

Potential mechanisms:

- share result card
- invite friend to free mini tryout
- event referral
- reward only when economics and abuse prevention are defined

Do not launch financial referral rewards before fraud/abuse rules are specified.

## 15. Conversion metrics

Initial metrics:

- landing → useful action
- useful action → registration
- registration → first completed practice
- first result → next recommended action
- package view → payment start
- payment start → payment success
- paid activation → first premium activity
- D1 / D7 return
- tryout completion rate
- recommendation click-through

Raw follower/reach metrics are secondary to qualified product activation and retention.

---

## 16. Launch sequence

Do not run aggressive acquisition before core P0 experience is coherent.

Recommended sequence:

1. language + design foundation
2. public landing conversion
3. auth/onboarding
4. CPNS/TKA core learning loop
5. payment clarity
6. analytics instrumentation
7. soft marketing tests
8. funnel measurement
9. CRO iteration
10. scale content/campaign spend only after conversion baseline exists
